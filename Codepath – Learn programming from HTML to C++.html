Frequently asked questions

Do I need to know math to program?

Not at first. Basic arithmetic and simple logic are enough; advanced math is only needed in fields such as graphics or machine learning.

How much time should I spend each day?

Thirty to sixty minutes of regular practice is worth more than one long session once a week.

Can I get a job knowing only these languages?

One language learned deeply plus real portfolio projects makes it easier; reading alone is not enough—build things.

Do I need to learn all eight languages?

No. Pick one path, learn one language well, and the others come more easily because concepts such as loops and functions repeat everywhere.
